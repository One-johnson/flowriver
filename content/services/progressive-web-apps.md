title: Progressive Web Applications
tagline: App-like experiences that install from the browser
description: >-
  Web apps that install on a phone's home screen, load fast on slow networks
  and keep working offline, with no app store needed.
longDescription: >-
  A progressive web app (PWA) gives your users most of what a native app
  offers, including a home-screen icon, offline access and push notifications,
  from a single codebase that also runs in any browser. You skip app store
  approval and fees, and every update reaches users the moment you publish it.
icon: AppWindow
features:
  - Installable on Android, iOS and desktop
  - Offline access and background sync
  - Push notifications
  - Fast loading on slow or patchy connections
  - One codebase for web and mobile
approach:
  - We decide together which features need to work offline and which can wait for a connection.
  - The app is designed mobile-first, then scaled up for tablets and desktops.
  - I test on low-end Android phones and slow networks, not just new devices on office Wi-Fi.
  - After launch I monitor performance and ship updates without users having to reinstall.

faqs:
  - question: What is the difference between a PWA and a native app?
    answer: >-
      A native app is built separately for iOS and Android and installed from
      an app store. A PWA is built once with web technology and installed
      straight from the browser. For most business tools, booking systems and
      content apps, a PWA does the job at a lower cost.
  - question: Can a PWA work without internet?
    answer: >-
      Yes. I cache the screens and data your users need most, so they can
      keep working offline. Changes sync back once they're connected again.
  - question: Can you turn our existing website into a PWA?
    answer: >-
      Often, yes. I review your current site and tell you whether it can be
      upgraded or would be better rebuilt.
